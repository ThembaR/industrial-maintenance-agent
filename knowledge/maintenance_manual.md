# Hydraulic Pump Maintenance Manual

## Error E17

Error E17 indicates that hydraulic pressure is below the expected
operating range.

### Possible causes

- Low hydraulic fluid level
- Restricted hydraulic filter
- Pump wear
- Hydraulic leakage
- Pressure sensor issue

### Recommended checks

1. Verify the current hydraulic pressure.
2. Check the hydraulic fluid level.
3. Inspect the hydraulic filter.
4. Inspect the system for visible leakage.
5. Verify the pressure sensor reading.
6. If pressure remains below the normal operating range,
   escalate to a qualified maintenance engineer.

## Normal operating pressure

Normal hydraulic pump pressure is approximately:

180-200 bar

Pressure below 160 bar should be investigated.

## Temperature

Normal operating temperature:

40-70 degrees Celsius

Temperature above 80 degrees Celsius requires investigation.

## Safety

The AI assistant must not instruct a technician to bypass
safety systems or perform unsafe maintenance procedures.

If insufficient information is available, the issue should
be escalated to a qualified maintenance engineer.
